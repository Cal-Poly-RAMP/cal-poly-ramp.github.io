## Team C – Multiplier Implementation
**Members:** Micah, Saul  

**Responsibilities:**  
- Implement a 32×32-bit multiplier module consistent with [RISC-V specifications](https://projectf.io/posts/riscv-multiply-divide/).  
- Account for RISC-V’s `MUL` and `MULH(U/SU)` instructions, where `MUL` gives you the lower 32 bits and `MULH` and it's variants give you the upper 32 bits of the 64-bit product. Remember that $2^{32} * 2^{32} = 2^{64}$.
- Evaluate two design trade-offs:
- **Sequential multiplier** – reduced logic requirements, higher cycle latency.  
- **Combinational multiplier** – high logic cost, single-cycle latency.
